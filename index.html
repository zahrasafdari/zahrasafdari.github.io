Zahra Safdari Fesaghandis

M.Sc. Student in Computer Engineering · Expected July 2027

Bilkent University, Ankara, Türkiye · ASAP Lab, advised by Prof. Sinem Sav

At Bilkent, I work with Prof. Sinem Sav on the security and privacy of AI systems. My projects cover LLM security and jailbreak attacks, adversarial attacks on vision-language models, and genomic data privacy, where I develop adaptive reinforcement-learning-based defenses as part of a TÜBİTAK 1001-funded project.

I have also worked with Prof. Suman Kalyan Maity at Missouri University of Science and Technology as a remote research assistant on multilingual hate speech detection and counterspeech generation. This work led to ParsCN, a Persian counter-narrative dataset published at ICWSM 2026, and a survey accepted at ACM Computing Surveys.

I received my B.Sc. in Computer Science from the University of Tabriz in 2024.

Research Projects

Current and recent projects. Work in progress is described at a high level.

  • Multimodal NLP

    Image-Based Counterspeech Generation

    Studying harmful image–text content and developing context-aware approaches to multimodal counterspeech.

  • LLM Security

    LLM Security and Jailbreak Attacks

    Investigating jailbreak attacks on large language models and security challenges in LLM-based agents, with a focus on understanding and evaluating unsafe model behavior.

  • Adversarial ML

    Adversarial Attacks on Vision-Language Models

    Examining how adversarial changes to visual inputs affect the safety and robustness of vision-language models. Related project: NOVA (Noise Oracle for Vision-Language Model Attacks).

  • Privacy

    Genomic Data Privacy and Adaptive RL Defenses

    Studying coordinated membership inference attacks against genomic beacons and developing adaptive reinforcement-learning-based defenses that balance data utility and individual privacy.

    • TÜBİTAK 1001

  • Social NLP

    FRAG: Fact-Grounded Counterspeech via Retrieval-Augmented Generation

    Exploring semantic retrieval and evidence-grounded generation to produce factual, context-aware counter-narratives.

  • Adversarial ML

    Adversarial Robustness of Generative Inpainting Models

    A course research project (2026) investigating test-time adversarial vulnerabilities in generative image inpainting and uncertainty-aware defense mechanisms.
